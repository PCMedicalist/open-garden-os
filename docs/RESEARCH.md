## Texas wheat varieties 2026 — NASS + Texas A&M AgriLife

[Source PDF](https://www.nass.usda.gov/sites/default/files/Statistics_by_State/Texas/Publications/Current_News_Release/2026_Rls/tx-wheat-variety-nass-2026%20final.pdf), producers surveyed Dec 2025–Jan 2026. TAM 204 moved from 5th to 1st at 6.1% of planted acres; TAM 112 second (4.4%); TAM 114 and TAM 111 tied third (4.1%); TAM 115 (3.2%), OK Corral (2.8%), Winterhawk (2.7%), Green Hammer (2.4%), Showdown (2.1%), Smith's Gold (1.6%). Classes: Hard Red Winter 78.1%, Hard Red Spring 5.5%, Soft Red Winter 1.2%, White 0.2%, Unknown 15.0%.

## Home gardens — Texas A&M AgriLife Extension

[Texas Home Vegetable Gardening Guide](https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/library/gardening/texas-home-vegetable-gardening-guide/) (Joseph Masabni): ~1 in 3 families gardens, mostly urban; Texas gardeners can produce year-round; choose a site with full/nearly-full sun, deep well-drained fertile soil, near water but away from competing shrubs/trees; select crops using the guide's small/large garden table.

[Easy Gardening: Cole Crops](https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/asset-external/easy-gardening-cole-crops/) — cool-season crop sheet.

Varieties modelled in the synthetic fixture: cole crops, tomato, pepper, bean, lettuce. **Suitability is county- and season-specific — check your local Extension office before planting.**

## Rotation proposal

The sources above publish what was planted, not a rotation schedule. Any rotation sequence in our data is an explicit modelling proposal for a digital twin, to be validated against local Extension guidance. Do not treat it as a field recommendation.
